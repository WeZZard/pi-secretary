# Owner Decisions

**Document type:** Decision log.

**Scope:** This log records decisions that the owner made. Each entry states the decision, the reason given, and the design section that carries it out. Decisions recorded in another repository, such as relay decision D3 in `relay-driver/docs/decisions.md`, are cited where they are used and are not copied here.

| PS-D10 | 2026-09-27 | Lifecycle failures in the relay stack are fixed reproducer first. Fixes stay on a branch in each repository until the owner reviews them; nothing is pushed or published. A fix that would change a documented behavior of another project, such as keeping the VM after a failed finish or the 6 h grace period, gets a reproducer only, and the behavior change waits for the owner's decision. | On 2026-09-26, VMs were held for up to 20.7 h after their runs ended, and the causes spanned four repositories.
